# First demand scan — 2026-09-19

Verdict: viable but crowded. AI jump detection is NOT a moat (OOFSkate owns it with USFS endorsement). The open gap is a cheap all-in-one progress tracker for learn-to-skate families + adult skaters.

## Competitors found
- **OOFSkate** (MIT spinout) — AI jump classification, height/rotation/airtime, GOE prediction. $19.99/mo, $199.99/yr. 4.7★ / 12 ratings. Official U.S. Figure Skating partner since Dec 2025.
- **Skate Score: AI Coach & Judge** (Android) — IJS-rules AI scoring, 33-point pose, live voice cues.
- **Learn To Skate USA** (official app) — 2.7★ / 29 ratings, "way too basic", last updated Jan 2025. ← weak incumbent
- **Figure It Out: Learn to Skate** — 18 tiers, 130+ skills, video tagging, no AI. $2.99/mo. Launched Jan 2026. Closest overlap.
- **IceLog Skating** — coach scheduling/parent dashboard, free.
- **Skater-Stats** — competition results, 5.0★ / 179 ratings.
- **Hockey AI-Analyzer** — AI clip feedback, $5/mo, 4.5★ / 73.
- **OnForm** (absorbed Hudl Technique; Coach's Eye died 2022) — coach video tool $19.99–59.99/mo.
- **NHL Sense Arena** — VR, $33–59/mo.

## Market size
- US Figure Skating: 246k+ members, 185,581 Learn to Skate USA, 1,000+ LTS programs.
- USA Hockey 2025-26: 586,002 players (record), 183,116 adults, 68,792 coaches.

## Spend
- Hockey >$2,500/yr/family; figure private coaching $50–120/hr.

## Tester channels
- r/hockeyplayers (~297k, adult rec), r/FigureSkating (~123k, mostly fans), adult figure skating FB groups, LTS program directors / coaches (1 coach → 10–30 families).

## Red flags
- Tiny review counts across the niche = small app-paying market so far.
- Minors' video → COPPA/privacy.
- Figure + hockey + LTS + coaches at once is too broad.

## Suggested
- First segment: LTS families + adult rec skaters, via coaches. Test ~$59–79/yr or per-season pass; free Pro for coaches.
