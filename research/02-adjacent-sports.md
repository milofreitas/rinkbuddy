# Adjacent sports: self-serve AI video coaching outside ice — 2026-09-19

Question: which self-serve AI coaching products in other sports worked, which failed, and what RinkBuddy should copy. Read alongside `00-first-scan.md` (OOFSkate owns AI jump detection plus the USFS endorsement; the open gap is a cheap progress tracker for learn-to-skate families and adult skaters).

---

## 1. Product-by-product scan

### Skiing: Carv (the closest analog: a winter sport with a seasonal, self-coached, adult-heavy user base)
- **What users do:** clip motion sensors to their boot straps; the phone stays in a pocket. All analysis runs in the app and works offline ([getcarv.com/sensors](https://getcarv.com/sensors)). **Carv now also offers a phone-only mode with no sensors, with 3 free trial days, where the phone in a pocket still produces Ski:IQ scores and coaching per run** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/carv-digital-ski-coach/id1238683093)).
- **Output:** Ski:IQ, one number from 0 to 200 (the average on-piste skier scores about 100) built from Balance, Edging, Rotary and Pressure, shown after every run and tracked over time ([Carv Ski:IQ](https://getcarv.com/blog/skiiq)). It also gives real-time audio coaching as you ski ([SKI Magazine](https://www.skimag.com/performance/instruction/carv-digital-ski-coach/)) at three levels: Track, Learn (tips between runs) and Train (live in-run audio) ([Carv product page](https://getcarv.com/products/carv)). Other features: drills at progressive levels and leaderboards by globe, country and mountain ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/carv-digital-ski-coach/id1238683093)).
- **Price:** $249/yr with the hardware included, or $129 for the devices plus $119/yr for 6 days of use ([Carv membership types](https://getcarv.com/blog/membership-types)). It also sells a day pass ([Carv daily pass](https://getcarv.com/products/carv-membership-dailypass)).
- **Traction:** 4.8★ from 2.8K ratings ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/carv-digital-ski-coach/id1238683093)). The homepage claims 56,967+ skiers ([getcarv.com](https://getcarv.com/)). About $20.8M raised, with a Series A in April 2024 ([Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/carv)).
- **Complaints:** a confusing UI, a robotic and repetitive voice, and trouble cancelling subscriptions ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/carv-digital-ski-coach/id1238683093)).

### Tennis and pickleball: SwingVision
- **What users do:** prop up a phone and record a match. The single-camera AI returns automatic stats, highlights and line calls ([swing.vision](https://swing.vision/home/)). On Apple Watch you can challenge a line call with a slow-motion replay ([SwingVision guide](https://swing.vision/guides/challenge-line-calls)).
- **Price:** Plus is $95.99/yr, Pro $179.99/yr ($24.99/mo) and Max $299.99/yr ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/swingvision-tennis-pickleball/id989461317)).
- **Traction:** 4.7★ from 4,900+ ratings, plus Apple Editors' Choice ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/swingvision-tennis-pickleball/id989461317)). A $6M Series A with Lindsay Davenport among the investors, used by more than 100 NCAA D1 teams ([SportsPro](https://www.sportspro.com/news/swingvision-ai-tennis-app-pickleball-padel/)).
- **Complaints:** shot labels are wrong ("calls everything a flat shot"), auto-scoring gets points wrong, auto-generated scores are hard to edit, and the paywall feels aggressive ([App Store reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/swingvision-tennis-pickleball/id989461317)).

### Golf: Sportsbox AI, V1, Mustard Golf, 18Birdies
- **Sportsbox AI** turns one phone video into a 3D skeleton and measures chest rotation, hip sway and spine tilt ([GolfLink](https://www.golflink.com/equipment/sportsbox-ai-review)). The free tier allows 5 swings/month; the paid tier is about $110/yr ([GolfLink](https://www.golflink.com/equipment/sportsbox-ai-review)). It raised a $5.5M seed led by EP Golf Ventures, a PGA of America vehicle ([SportsPro](https://www.sportspro.com/news/sportsbox-ai-seed-round-golf/)). **In April 2026 an investor group led by Bryson DeChambeau bought it in an "eight-figure" deal**, after DeChambeau credited it for swing changes behind his 2024 US Open win. An agentic AI assistant, "SAMI", is planned ([SportsPro](https://www.sportspro.com/news/technology/bryson-dechambeau-sportsbox-ai-acquisition-april-2026/), [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260407643695/en/Bryson-DeChambeau-led-Group-Acquires-Sportsbox-AI-Announces-SAMI-the-Next-Generation-of-Agentic-AI-Coaching)).
- **V1 Golf** costs $9.99/mo or $69.99/yr, with pro model swings, ball tracing and skeletal tracking ([V1 Sports](https://v1sports.com/athletes/buy-v1-golf-app/)). The real business is **V1 COACH**: a free athlete app, with the coach paying for a branded experience and AI-written follow-ups "in their voice" ([V1 COACH](https://v1sports.com/coaches/v1-coach-app/)).
- **Mustard Golf** was built with Golf Digest, Justin Rose and top teacher Mark Blackburn. Users see their top swing fault for free; paid is $24.99/mo or $149.99/yr ([WBD press](https://press.wbd.com/us/media-release/golf-digest/golf-digest-mustard-launch-groundbreaking-ai-powered-platform-mustard-golf-help-every)).
- **18Birdies** launched an AI swing analyzer in 2020. It gives a swing score, compares the swing with pro models, and prescribes drills, all bundled into an existing premium golf GPS/scorecard subscription ([GolfWRX](https://www.golfwrx.com/615154/18birdies-launches-ai-coach-golfs-first-video-based-swing-analyzer/)).

### Basketball: HomeCourt (NEX), Ballogy
- **HomeCourt** counts and charts shots in real time using only an iPhone camera ([NEX press](https://www.homecourt.ai/press-releases/2018/7/13/nex-team-inc-debuts-with-4-million-in-funding-and-launches-homecourt-the-first-ai-powered-basketball-training-app)). Pricing is $7.99/mo or $69.99/yr, the subscription can be shared with up to 4 family members, and **team plans run $1.50/player/month while creating a team is free for coaches** ([HomeCourt pricing](https://www.homecourt.ai/pricing)). The NBA took equity in 2019 and added NBA-branded challenges, badges and drills with Steve Nash ([NBA.com](https://www.nba.com/news/nba-nex-team-homecourt-app-partnership)). The company raised a $25M Series B ([SaaS News](https://www.thesaasnews.com/news/entertainment-startup-nex-raises-25-million-in-series-b/)).
- **Ballogy** has AI-verified skill metrics, a shareable "digital athletic resume" visible to 2,000+ colleges, and leaderboards. It claims 300,000+ athletes, coaches and teams ([Ballogy relaunch, Aug 2026](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260819233592/en/Ballogy-Brings-AI-Powered-Player-Development-to-Every-Athlete-With-Launch-of-All-New-App)).

### Baseball: Mustard, PitchAI, Uplift Labs, Rapsodo
- **Mustard** (pitching) produces a "report card" from phone video. Investors include Drew Brees and Nolan Ryan, and the company has raised $6M in total ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/01/18/mustard-looks-beyond-baseball-as-it-raises-another-3-75m-for-sensor-free-sports-coaching/)).
- **PitchAI (ProPlayAI)** measures sensorless pitching biomechanics from any smartphone, gives 3D comparisons against other pitchers, and flags fatigue and stress. Driveline distributes it ([ProPlayAI](https://proplayai.com/pitchai/), [Driveline](https://www.drivelinebaseball.com/pitchai/)).
- **Uplift Labs** does markerless 3D capture from two iPhones and partners with Driveline. It has raised about $10.7M ([uplift.ai](https://www.uplift.ai/sports/baseball), [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/uplift/__9O2SH6hMU_XlLrSeD5SlZoynUoO72u497BHafpmgbhc)).
- **Rapsodo** is hardware (radar/camera), not phone-only. Even so, its golf unit still charges a $199.99/yr membership on top of the device ([Rapsodo](https://rapsodo.com/pages/rapsodo-golf-mlm2pro-premium-membership)).

### Running, soccer, fitness, judged sports and long-tail sports
- **Ochy (running gait):** phone video in, results in under 60 seconds covering foot strike, vertical oscillation and ground contact time ([ochy.io](https://www.ochy.io/runners)). **adidas gives it away to adiClub members for loyalty points** ([Ochy × adidas](https://www.ochy.io/blog/adidas-and-ochy-bringing-ai-powered-gait-analysis-to-runners-worldwide)).
- **aiScout (soccer):** free for players, who film 75 standardized drills that the AI scores. Clubs (Chelsea, Burnley) and MLS pay to search the database. It has about 100k players, and 135 have been trialed or signed ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2024/03/01/tech/aiscout-app-soccer-scouting-spc-intl)).
- **Onyx (fitness):** a phone camera counts reps, corrects form and gives real-time audio. It held 4.9★ from about 2,500 ratings ([Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/living/innovative-workout-app-onyx-corrects-your-form-and-counts/351459)) and was acquired by Cure.fit in January 2021 ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/curefit-acquires-digital-fitness-company-onyx-aims-to-accelerate-international-offering-301212500.html)).
- **Kemtai:** camera-based form feedback that pivoted to **B2B** physical therapy and digital health: 111 tracked points, 2,000+ exercises, and a claimed 30% cut in in-person PT visits in an HMO pilot ([Kemtai](https://kemtai.com/product/computer-vision-rehab/)).
- **Tempo:** raised about $398M ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/tempo/__MprfUYMasaeKMrJrA3D7B_IcBGSl1q5FDwEEDAO6SVc)). Reviewers call its form feedback "inconsistent" and say it misses nuance ([Balanced Brawn](https://balancedbrawn.com/2025/08/21/tempo-fit-review/)). It went through layoff rounds ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Tempo-layoff-Reviews-EI_IE4399939.0,5_KH6,12.htm)).
- **Gymnastics: Fujitsu JSS** has been co-developed with FIG since 2017. It switched from sensors to camera-only in 2023 and was used on all 10 apparatuses at the 2023 Worlds ([Fujitsu](https://info.archives.global.fujitsu/global/about/resources/news/press-releases/2023/1005-02.html)). It is federation-grade judging support, not a consumer app.
- **Dance: STEEZY** gives no AI scoring. The value is in the player: mirror mode, looping, speed control, and a webcam view of yourself next to the instructor ([STEEZY](https://www.steezy.co/posts/start-here)).
- **Climbing, martial arts and swimming** are full of new single-developer "AI coach" apps. Examples: Climbah ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/climbah-bouldering-climb-ai/id6755648466)) and JAB AI ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.limitnil.jabai&hl=en_US)) come from the same developer (limitnil); Karate AI and Taekwondo AI score techniques 0–100 ([Titans Grip](https://www.titans-grip.com/karate/)). Swimming has FINIS LaneVision for velocity and stroke rate ([Swimming World](https://www.swimmingworldmagazine.com/news/introducing-finis-lanevision-the-worlds-first-ai-computer-vision-swim-training-technology/)). None has visible scale. The "Skate Score" Android app in our first scan fits this template-app pattern.

## 2. Patterns that drive paid adoption

1. **One headline score that moves.** Carv's Ski:IQ (0–200, average 100, shown after every run) ([Carv](https://getcarv.com/blog/skiiq)), 18Birdies' swing score ([GolfWRX](https://www.golfwrx.com/615154/18birdies-launches-ai-coach-golfs-first-video-based-swing-analyzer/)) and the 0–100 martial-arts scores all do this. Users pay to watch a number they can beat go up. A long list of metrics is not enough.
2. **Zero-effort capture that produces something to share.** SwingVision's automatic stats, highlights and line calls from one propped-up phone ([swing.vision](https://swing.vision/home/)) and HomeCourt's automatic shot charts pay off even when the user never asks for coaching. The recording itself becomes the reward.
3. **Coaching while you perform, not a report afterwards.** Carv's in-run audio ([SKI](https://www.skimag.com/performance/instruction/carv-digital-ski-coach/)) and Onyx's real-time voice cues ([Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/living/innovative-workout-app-onyx-corrects-your-form-and-counts/351459)) drew the strongest reviews ("did not ski one day without it", per the [Carv App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/carv-digital-ski-coach/id1238683093)).
4. **Borrowed authority from elite athletes or federations.** Sportsbox had PGA money and then DeChambeau; Mustard had Justin Rose and Golf Digest; HomeCourt had NBA equity; aiScout has Premier League clubs; Fujitsu has FIG. On ice, OOFSkate already holds the USFS endorsement ([00-first-scan](./00-first-scan.md)).
5. **Coach and team channels priced per seat, free to the coach.** HomeCourt: teams free to create, $1.50/player/month ([pricing](https://www.homecourt.ai/pricing)). V1: the athlete app is free and the coach pays ([V1 COACH](https://v1sports.com/coaches/v1-coach-app/)).

## 3. What has NOT been done on ice: feature ideas for RinkBuddy

- **"Skate:IQ", one progress score per session** for learn-to-skate and adult skaters. It would be built from edge quality, knee bend, posture and stroke symmetry, and tied to the Learn to Skate USA badge levels. OOFSkate scores jumps; nobody scores a Basic 3 skater's stroking.
- **Live audio cues through an earbud during a public session**, Carv-style ("bend your knees", "push to the side, not the back"). The phone would stay on the boards while the skater loops the rink. Ice has no equivalent of in-run coaching today.
- **Automatic session highlights** (SwingVision's pattern): a 30-second reel of the clean crossovers or the first backward glide that a parent can send to grandparents. This is the free-tier hook and the viral loop.
- **Badge-test readiness check:** film the required elements and get "ready / not ready" for each element against the official level checklist. This mirrors aiScout's standardized drills scored the same way for everyone.
- **Rink leaderboards and challenges**, like Carv's leaderboards by country and mountain ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/carv-digital-ski-coach/id1238683093)) and HomeCourt's NBA challenges. Examples: "longest one-foot glide at your rink this month", and for hockey, crossover laps timed from video.
- **Hockey skating stride score:** stride width, recovery and knee flexion from one side-on video, the PitchAI/Ochy biomechanics model applied to hockey skating rather than jumps.
- **Family plan:** HomeCourt shares one subscription across 4 family members ([pricing](https://www.homecourt.ai/pricing)), which fits families with several kids in learn-to-skate.

## 4. Business-model lessons

- **Price points cluster at $70–$180/yr** for phone-only consumer apps: HomeCourt $69.99, V1 $69.99, Sportsbox about $110, Mustard $149.99, SwingVision Pro $179.99 (sources above). Anything that needs hardware lands at $199–$249/yr (Carv, Rapsodo). The $59–79/yr test in `00-first-scan` sits at the bottom of the phone-only band, which leaves room for a higher "Pro" tier later.
- **Freemium with a hard free cap works:** 5 swings/month (Sportsbox), 300 shots/month (HomeCourt), one free top fault (Mustard). Give away the diagnosis and charge for tracking progress over time.
- **Phone-only beats hardware for acquisition, and hardware leaders are converging on phone-only too:** Carv added a sensor-free mode ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/carv-digital-ski-coach/id1238683093)) and Fujitsu dropped sensors in 2023 ([Fujitsu](https://info.archives.global.fujitsu/global/about/resources/news/press-releases/2023/1005-02.html)). RinkBuddy should not build hardware.
- **The B2B2C coach channel is where retention lives.** V1's coach-pays model, HomeCourt team plans, Ballogy's college reach and Kemtai's move to clinics all point the same way: the coach or institution drives repeat use. RinkBuddy should give coaches the tool for free and let them add families at a per-skater rate.
- **Partner layering:** Ochy got distribution through adidas loyalty points ([Ochy](https://www.ochy.io/blog/adidas-and-ochy-bringing-ai-powered-gait-analysis-to-runners-worldwide)), and Sportsbox got its money through the PGA of America. With USFS taken by OOFSkate, the equivalents to pursue are rink operators, skate brands (sharpening loyalty programs) and USA Hockey's adult programs.

## 5. Cautionary tales

- **Coach's Eye (TechSmith) shut down in September 2022** because TechSmith chose to put its resources elsewhere ([SnookerZone](https://snookerzone.co.uk/coachs-eye-app-will-cease-in-september-2022/), [VisualEyes](https://www.visualeyesapp.com/coaches-eye-alternative)). **Hudl sold Technique to OnForm in 2021** ([OnForm](https://onform.com/blog/onform-acquires-hudl-technique/)). The lesson is that manual video tools (slow motion, drawing) are a commodity large companies walk away from. The value has to come from automatic AI output.
- **HomeCourt's phone-to-hardware pivot:** retention was 3x better on iPad and 10x better when the phone or iPad was connected to a TV. The founder concluded they had been "building for the wrong platform" and built the Nex Playground console ([The Game Business](https://www.thegamebusiness.com/p/we-want-a-nex-playground-in-every)), which sold about 600k units in 2025 ([YSBR](https://youthsportsbusinessreport.com/camera-based-gaming-console-nex-playground-sells-600000-units-in-2025-outsells-xbox-during-black-friday/)). A standalone phone training app for kids had weak retention. RinkBuddy should anchor usage to scheduled ice time and to a coach or parent, not rely on kids opening the app by themselves.
- **Tempo raised about $398M, and its form AI was still called inconsistent** ([Balanced Brawn](https://balancedbrawn.com/2025/08/21/tempo-fit-review/)). SwingVision's worst reviews are also about accuracy ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/swingvision-tennis-pickleball/id989461317)). Wrong AI output hurts more than missing features, so RinkBuddy should let users correct labels and should hedge its confidence (for example, "looks like a waltz jump").
- **Pivots toward B2B:** Kemtai moved from consumer fitness to physical therapy and digital health, and Onyx sold to Cure.fit. Standalone consumer form-feedback apps struggle to stay independent.
- **Template-app flooding:** one developer ships the same "AI Coach" shell across climbing, martial arts and more (limitnil). Low review counts across the ice niche (see `00-first-scan`) may partly be this noise. Differentiation has to come from the curriculum (the LTS level map) and the coach network, not from saying "AI".
