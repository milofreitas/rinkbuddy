### Description
RinkBuddy turns the video on your phone into a record of what you can actually do on the ice.

Film a few seconds of yourself skating, tag the moment you did the skill, and RinkBuddy keeps it: the date, how it went, a still and a short clip of the attempt itself. Months later you can open any skill and see your first attempt beside your latest one.

TAG WHAT YOU DID
Pick the skill before you record, then mark the moment it happens. Each tag counts toward that skill's progress. Tag the falls too — they are part of the record.

SEE THE SKILL BEFORE YOU TRY IT
Every skill shows the pattern your blade leaves on the ice, what the movement should look like, the key points to focus on, and a tutorial when you want one.

FOLLOW A REAL CURRICULUM
Skills are organised the way skating is actually taught, across three disciplines: learn-to-skate foundations, figure skating and hockey skating. Skills unlock as you land the ones they build on.

WATCH YOURSELF IMPROVE
First tried, first clean, mastered — with dates. Your progress record syncs to your account, so it survives a new phone even though the full videos stay on your device.

AI SKILL SUGGESTIONS (Pro)
Scan a video and RinkBuddy suggests what it saw, attempt by attempt. You confirm or correct each one — nothing is added to your progress until you do.

KEY FEATURES
- Record in the app, or import video shot in your phone's camera, including slow motion
- Frame-by-frame review at the clip's real frame rate
- Tag skills yourself, or review AI suggestions (Pro)
- A short clip kept for every confirmed attempt
- Milestones, success rate and a skills radar
- Ice-trace diagrams and key points for every core skill
- Works at the rink without a connection; AI scanning needs one

FREE AND PRO
Free: 3 videos, full skill tracking and tagging.
Pro ($9.99/month): unlimited videos and AI skill suggestions.
